# Style: authority

Numbers live in `style.json`. This file holds the judgement calls.

## The look in one line
A confident person talking straight to you, words appearing as they say them, one yellow word that carries the point.

## Rules
- Captions are **2–4 words**, centred at ~55% height (chest level), never covering the mouth.
- **One keyword per 1–2 sentences**, on its own line under the chunk, yellow, 1.5× size. Make it UPPERCASE and 2× size
  only for the 1–2 biggest words of the reel ("REFRAME", "BUDGET"), at most one every 10 s.
- **Listicle header** (number big yellow italic + title white italic caps) sits top-left for the whole point.
- **Hard cuts only.** Energy comes from punch-ins (1.12×) on every jump cut and slow push-ins on the hook and each point's title.
- **Cutaways are short** (1–2 s) and literal: show the noun being said. Captions keep running over them.
- **Icon pops:** when a brand, tool or object is named, pop its icon above the head in a dashed yellow box (from the
  user's own assets or a free icon set; never a real company's logo without the user's OK).
- **Stage scene** (optional, once per reel): dark gradient background + one cut-out object + text grey → yellow
  ("Emotion opens / the wallet").
- **End:** Follow-button pop with the user's handle.
- Grade is clean and natural. Don't push the look; skin must stay true.

## Don't
- Movie clips (the references use them; copyright strikes).
- More than 2 transitions per reel.
- Keyword on every chunk: it stops meaning anything.

## CapCut template: `STYLE authority`
The user makes this once (see CARA_GUNA.md). It must contain: the caption text box ("caption KEYWORD", two colours),
the header text ("1 TITLE"), a pop-in text animation, the adjustment layer with this grade, and one zoom-in transition.
